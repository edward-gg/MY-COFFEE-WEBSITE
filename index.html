Our Story

A Neighbourhood Coffee Shop, Online

Bean Boutique began as a small counter on the corner of a busy street. Today we roast and sell our own single-origin and blended beans, stock the equipment we use every day, and host workshops for anyone who wants to learn the craft.

This website is a first step towards bringing that experience to a wider audience — browse our coffee, brew along with us, and join the community.
